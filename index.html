Treatment starts with understanding the person.

Rather than addressing isolated symptoms, acupuncture examines how your body's systems interact. We listen attentively to your medical history, assess bodily cues through traditional pulse and tongue diagnosis, and explain every step of the therapeutic process before treatment begins.
