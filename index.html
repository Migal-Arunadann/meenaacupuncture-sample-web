02 / Neuromuscular Calming

Headaches, tension, and persistent sleep issues.

Persistent mental strain and fatigue disrupt the autonomic nervous system, leading to vascular constriction and restless sleep. Acupuncture works directly on vagus nerve pathways to down-regulate sympathetic fight-or-flight tension and induce restorative rest.

Chronic Migraines Tension Headaches Insomnia & Sleep Latency Anxiety Tension Mental Exhaustion

Clinical Methodology

Calming cranial and meridian points (such as Yintang and Shenmen) quiet overactive neurological loops and stabilize blood vessel tone.

Patient Experience

Deep relaxation during quiet 30-minute needle retention; many patients sleep peacefully throughout the session.

03 / Neuro Rehabilitation

Nerve regeneration, Bell's palsy, and numbness.

Nerve injuries, facial paralysis, or post-stroke complications require stimulating micro-current pathways to awaken dormant muscle groups and restore neuromuscular communication. Dr. Sakthivel applies dedicated protocols designed to gently re-educate facial and peripheral nerves.

Bell's Palsy / Facial Palsy Peripheral Neuropathy Post-Stroke Motor Rehab Tingling in Extremities

Clinical Methodology

Targeted motor point stimulation to reconnect peripheral nerve impulses and stimulate circulation to dormant tissues.

Care Commitment

Structured progressive courses tailored to patient recovery timelines, working collaboratively with their medical care.

Traditional acupuncture assesses the body as an interconnected constitutional ecosystem. Sluggish digestion, acid reflux, or persistent lack of energy are treated by balancing the stomach and spleen meridians to restore nutrient assimilation and bodily vigor.
