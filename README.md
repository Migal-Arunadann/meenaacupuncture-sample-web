# Meena Acupuncture Clinic · மீனா குத்தூசி சிகிச்சை மருத்துவகம்

A bespoke, hand-crafted four-page website for **Meena Acupuncture Clinic**, located at Platinum Tower, Srinivasan Nagar, Kolathur, Chennai.

---

## Art Direction & Visual Concept: "Quiet Precision"

Designed around the philosophy:
> **Chennai neighbourhood clinic × quiet Japanese editorial design × contemporary Indian healthcare × natural materials.**

- **Paper & Ink Palette**: Warm uncoated paper (`#F3EFE6`), Ink (`#202522`), Deep Leaf (`#30483F`), Warm White (`#FCFAF5`), Soft Sage (`#BAC5B7`), and Clay (`#A66F4C`).
- **Restrained Typography**: Lora (Editorial Serif), DM Sans (Clean Modern Sans), and Noto Sans Tamil.
- **Graphic Motif**: Acupuncture point motif (`●───`) used sparingly as an editorial divider and journey indicator.
- **Bespoke Clinic Emblem**: Extracted and vectorized directly from the clinic's authentic signboard.
- **Zero AI-Slop & No Heavy Frameworks**: Pure semantic HTML5, CSS3, and Vanilla JavaScript.

---

## Site Architecture

1. **`index.html`** – Homepage (Editorial 55/45 hero, unhurried introduction, asymmetric clinic story, horizontal patient journey, Deep Leaf acupuncture feature, real patient reviews, location).
2. **`about.html`** – About the Practice (Two-column editorial story, Dr. Sakthivel practitioner profile, clinical philosophy, irregular clinic gallery).
3. **`acupuncture.html`** – Understanding Acupuncture (Long-form reading guide, pull quote, vertically numbered sequence, preparation checklist, responsible medical notice).
4. **`contact.html`** – Contact Us (Platinum Tower exterior, direct Call & WhatsApp options, verified Google Maps embed & link, clinic hours).
